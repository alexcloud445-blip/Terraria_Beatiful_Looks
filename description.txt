Beautiful Looks — a purely cosmetic mod: wings, auras, trails, halos and vanity outfits.

Press K (rebindable in Controls) to open the Looks menu:
- Aura: 10 styles with density, custom color (hue / saturation / brightness) and rainbow mode
- Trail: 6 movement trail styles
- Effects: halo, back glow and ground ring drawn on your character
- Wardrobe: 6 wings, 5 hats and 3 capes
- 3 preset slots, randomize and reset
Looks are saved per character and synced in multiplayer.
