Beautiful Looks — a purely cosmetic mod: auras, glowing trails, halos, magic circles and vanity outfits. No gameplay changes.

Press K (rebindable in Controls) to open the Looks menu:
- Aura: 17 styles on a custom glowing particle system (comets with tails, storm with lightning, prism shards, butterflies, runes, divine light and more) with density, size and speed sliders
- Trail: glowing shader ribbons (soft, rainbow, flame, Empress, twin spiral), 11 particle trails and phantom copies of your character
- Effects: 5 halo styles, glow, god rays, ground ring, magic sigil and pulse rings, with optional vanilla dye shaders
- Colors: two colors per part (plus rainbow) and 12 color themes
- Looks: 10 ready-made looks (Seraph, Inferno, Frost, Sakura, Void, Empress, Storm...)
- Wardrobe: 6 wings, 5 hats and 3 capes
- 5 preset slots, randomize and reset
Looks are saved per character and synced in multiplayer.
